What makes Vedic Astrology different from Western Astrology?

The primary difference lies in the astronomical calculation of the zodiac. Western astrology uses the Tropical Zodiac (Sayana), which is tied to the seasons and fixes the Vernal Equinox to 0° Aries. Due to the precession of the equinoxes, the Tropical zodiac drifts away from the actual star constellations by about 1 degree every 72 years.

Vedic Astrology (Jyotisha) uses the Sidereal Zodiac (Nirayana), which factors in an adjustment called the Ayanamsa (commonly the Lahiri/Chitra Paksha Ayanamsa). This ensures that when the engine says a planet is in Aries, it is physically aligned with the actual constellation of Aries in the night sky.

How does the engine predict exact life events and timings?

Event prediction in authentic Jyotisha is not based on guesswork; it relies on a multi-layered locking mechanism. Our engine cross-references three distinct systems to pinpoint life epochs:

  • Vimshottari Dasha: A 120-year mathematical timeline calculated from the exact degree of your birth Moon. It determines the overarching themes (Mahadasha) and specific localized events (Antardasha/Bhukti) active in your life.
  • Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) Transits: We track the exact degree-to-degree conjunctions of transiting slow planets (Jupiter/Saturn) against your natal planets to detect major karmic shifts.
  • Micro-Transits (Gochara): Once the Dasha and Nadi transits open a "window," fast-moving planets like the Sun, Mars, and Venus act as detonators. The engine scans for the exact day these planets hit sensitive degrees to predict manifestation dates.
What are Divisional Charts (Vargas) like the D9 Navamsa?

The D1 Rasi chart is the physical body of your destiny, but Vargas are the microscopic DNA. A planet may look exalted in the D1 chart, but if it is debilitated in a divisional chart, its inner strength is hollow.

Crucial Divisional Charts:

  • D9 Navamsa: The most important sub-chart. It reveals your true soul strength, the second half of your life, and the exact nature of your spouse and marriage.
  • D7 Saptamsha: The microscopic chart analyzed to predict the destiny, gender sequences, and karmic blocks regarding progeny (children).
  • D10 Dashamsha: The ultimate map of your career, public authority, and professional milestones.
What is Ashtakavarga and why does it matter for Transits?

Ashtakavarga is an incredibly advanced point-based mathematical system used to judge the absolute strength of transits (Gochara). It calculates how many "benefic points" (Bindus) a zodiac sign receives from the 7 physical planets and the Ascendant.

A sign with 28 or more SAV points creates a protective shield. For example, if you are undergoing a traditionally "bad" Saturn transit (like Sade Sati), but Saturn is moving through a sign where you have 35 Ashtakavarga points, the negative effects are mathematically neutralized, and the period may surprisingly yield massive success.

What is Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha) and does it ruin marriage?

Mangal Dosha occurs when Mars (Kuja) is placed in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house from the Ascendant or the Moon. It indicates an excess of fiery, aggressive, or protective energy that can cause friction in partnerships.

The Engine's Dosha Samya (Cancellation) Protocol:

Most basic software labels you "Manglik" and stops there. However, classical texts contain dozens of cancellation rules (Bhangas). Our engine dynamically evaluates these rules. If Mars is in its own sign, aspected by a strong Jupiter, or neutralized by age maturity (post 28 years), the engine will accurately report the dosha as Cancelled, meaning the marital friction is spiritually neutralized.

How does Synastry (Kundli Matching) work beyond just the 36 Gunas?

The traditional "Ashtakoota" 36-point system is only the surface layer of compatibility, relying entirely on the Moon Nakshatras. True astrological matching requires deep structural analysis, which our engine performs automatically:

  • Mutual Dosha Rectification: Does Person A's Jupiter cast a healing aspect on Person B's afflicted 7th house? If so, they physically heal each other's karmic flaws.
  • Nadi & Degree Overlays: We check if Person A's Venus exactly conjuncts Person B's Mars, creating undeniable physical chemistry, or if their D9 Lagnas overlap, indicating a fated soul contract.
  • Dasha Synchronization: Even with a perfect 36/36 score, a marriage will struggle if the couple's current operating Dasha periods are fundamentally opposed. The engine evaluates both timelines to ensure they are ready for union simultaneously.
What is Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) Astrology?

Unlike Parashari astrology which heavily relies on Houses (Bhavas) and Lordship, Bhrigu Nandi Nadi evaluates destiny purely through planetary conjunctions and alignments in the sky, regardless of the house they sit in.

In BNN, Jupiter represents the "Jiva" (the native's soul). If Jupiter transits over natal Venus, it guarantees a fated encounter with wealth or a partner. By mathematically sweeping the zodiac for these exact planetary collisions using a 2-degree orb, our engine achieves pinpoint accuracy for life's biggest milestones without needing the exact birth time down to the minute.
