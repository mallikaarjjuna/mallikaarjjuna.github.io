What is a Janam Kundli (Birth Chart)?

A Janam Kundli, or birth chart, is a precise snapshot of the celestial sky at the exact moment and geographic location of your birth. It maps out the positions of the 9 Grahas (planets) across the 12 Rashis (zodiac signs) and 12 Bhavas (houses).

In Vedic astrology, this chart acts as a cosmic blueprint, outlining your karmic potential, life path, strengths, and challenges based on the principles of the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra.

How is the Janma Nakshatra calculated?

The Janma Nakshatra is the specific lunar mansion (out of 27 possible Nakshatras) that the Moon was transiting through at the time of your birth. Each Nakshatra spans exactly 13 degrees and 20 minutes of the zodiac.

Why is it important?

The Nakshatra determines your starting Dasha (planetary period) and plays a critical role in mental temperament, emotional compatibility (Synastry), and determining the auspicious Nama-Akshara (starting letter) for naming a child.

What is the significance of the Lagna (Ascendant)?

The Lagna is the specific degree of the zodiac that was rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. Because the Earth rotates rapidly (roughly 1 degree every 4 minutes), an accurate birth time is crucial for determining the Lagna.

The Lagna represents the self, the physical body, and the overarching perspective through which you experience the world. It sets the baseline for the entire house system in your chart.
