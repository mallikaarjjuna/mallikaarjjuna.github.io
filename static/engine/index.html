How does the engine predict exact life events and timings?

Event prediction in authentic Jyotisha is not based on guesswork; it relies on a multi-layered locking mechanism. Our engine cross-references three distinct systems to pinpoint life epochs:

  • Vimshottari Dasha: A 120-year mathematical timeline calculated from the exact degree of your birth Moon.
  • Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) Transits: We track the exact degree-to-degree conjunctions of transiting slow planets (Jupiter/Saturn) against your natal planets to detect major karmic shifts.
  • Micro-Transits (Gochara): Once the Dasha and Nadi transits open a "window," fast-moving planets like the Sun act as detonators. The engine scans for the exact day these planets hit sensitive degrees to predict manifestation dates.
What makes Vedic Astrology different from Western Astrology?

Vedic Astrology (Jyotisha) uses the Sidereal Zodiac (Nirayana), which factors in an adjustment called the Ayanamsa. This ensures that when the engine says a planet is in Aries, it is physically aligned with the actual constellation of Aries in the night sky, unlike Western astrology which slowly drifts from the actual stars over time.

What are Divisional Charts (Vargas) like the D9 Navamsa?

The D1 Rasi chart is the physical body of your destiny, but Vargas are the microscopic DNA.

  • D9 Navamsa: The most important sub-chart. It reveals your true soul strength and the exact nature of your marriage.
  • D7 Saptamsha: Analyzed to predict the destiny and karmic blocks regarding progeny (children).
